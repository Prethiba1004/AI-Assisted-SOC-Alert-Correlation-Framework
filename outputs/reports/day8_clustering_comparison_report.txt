4. RESEARCH OBSERVATIONS
- DBSCAN identified one cluster containing two events.
- HDBSCAN classified all four events as noise.
- Both algorithms identified two events as noise in DBSCAN, while HDBSCAN identified all four as noise.
- Silhouette Score was not applicable because neither algorithm produced at least two non-noise clusters.

5. RESEARCH LIMITATIONS
- The experiment uses only four synthetic security events.
- Results are proof-of-concept observations.
- Larger datasets and parameter experiments are required.
- Noise events must be preserved for SOC analyst review.
- Similarity or clustering does not prove a common incident.

6. CONCLUSION
DBSCAN and HDBSCAN demonstrated different clustering behaviour on the current Sentence-BERT embeddings. Further experiments with larger datasets are required before drawing general performance conclusions.
