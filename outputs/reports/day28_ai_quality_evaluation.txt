DAY 28 - AI INVESTIGATION QUALITY EVALUATION
============================================

Project: AI-Assisted SOC Alert Correlation Framework
Date: 18 November 2026

1. OBJECTIVE
Evaluate the quality of RAG-assisted AI investigation outputs using
ground-truth findings and predefined quality criteria.

2. EVALUATION CRITERIA
Five criteria were evaluated, each scored from 0 to 2:
- Evidence accuracy
- Technical correctness
- MITRE ATT&CK relevance
- Investigation usefulness
- Clarity

Maximum score per investigation: 10.

3. EVALUATION RESULTS

META-001: 9/10 (90%)
META-002: 10/10 (100%)
META-003: 9/10 (90%)

Average score: 9.33/10
Average percentage: 93.33%

4. EVIDENCE REVIEW FINDINGS

META-001:
The AI used plural wording for failed-logon attempts, although
the source contained only one failed-logon event.

META-002:
No predefined evidence-attribution issue was detected.

META-003:
The source IP 192.168.1.50 belongs to Event 1. The AI incorrectly
attributed this IP to both authentication events. Event 2 did not
contain this source IP in the original normalized evidence.

5. MITRE ATT&CK AND TECHNICAL REVIEW
All three investigations preserved the original incident status
and MITRE mapping uncertainty.

Potential mappings remained potential. Retrieved MITRE techniques
were treated as candidates rather than confirmed mappings.
No incident was incorrectly promoted to confirmed status during
the reviewed assessments.

6. VALIDATION AND HUMAN REVIEW
All three investigations were evaluated.
All five scoring criteria were recorded for each investigation.
Human review is required for all investigations.

7. LIMITATIONS
- Evaluation used only three curated meta-alert cases.
- The evaluator uses predefined, rule-based checks.
- Some criteria use keyword-based scoring.
- The score does not establish real-world AI accuracy or
  generalization to unseen security incidents.
- Automated checks cannot verify every AI-generated statement.
- Human analyst review remains essential.

8. CONCLUSION
The Day 28 evaluation produced a preliminary average score of
93.33% across three curated investigations.

The review identified two evidence-quality issues:
incorrect plural wording in META-001 and incorrect source-IP
attribution in META-003.

The evaluator's MITRE preservation condition was corrected.
The revised evaluation successfully awarded META-001 a
technical correctness score of 2/2.

This evaluation improves visibility into AI investigation
quality while reinforcing evidence grounding, MITRE mapping
uncertainty, and human analyst oversight.
